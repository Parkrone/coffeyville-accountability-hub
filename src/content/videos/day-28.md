Day 28 of exposing AI data centers until my new hometown of Coffeyville passes a legal moratorium.

Breaking news from the High Plains Journal: Google is now actively prospecting Ford County in southwest Kansas as a landing spot for a massive new data center campus.

If you think these multi-billion-dollar tech giants aren't looking at small rural towns like Coffeyville, you need to understand the scale of what they are building across Kansas.

In Kansas City, Google signed an energy contract with Evergy for a 710-megawatt data center. To put that into perspective, 710 megawatts is more electricity than 500,000 Kansas homes consume combined.

A single facility draws that massive baseload continuously, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. To feed that power hunger, regional utility reports show power companies across the Midwest are delaying coal-fired power plant retirements and burning more coal just to keep baseline capacity online.

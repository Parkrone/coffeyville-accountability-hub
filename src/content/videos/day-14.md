Day 14 of exposing AI data centers until my new hometown of Coffeyville passes a legal moratorium.

Today we need to talk about the single biggest buzzword being used to sell data centers to small-town city leaders: closed-loop cooling.

Developers like to compare closed-loop systems to a car radiator. But unlike a car radiator, data center cooling loops require chemical treatment with biocides and corrosion inhibitors, and periodic blowdown flushes into municipal sewers.
